What is actually running

    
The force field, the topology parse, every force term and the integrator
    are validated against OpenMM — per-atom forces agree to 5.2×10⁻⁸. Those
    numbers and the commands that produce them are in
    docs/STATUS.md.

    
The GPU force path is complete: bonded, cell list,
    neighbour list, direct-space nonbonded and PME all run on your GPU and
    reproduce OpenMM's total energy to 1.5×10−6 and its
    per-atom forces to 8.8×10−5. The button above runs the
    bonded differential test on your own hardware; npm run test:gpu
    runs all five.

    
Minimisation, dynamics, the constraint solver and the barostat all run on
    the device, and a block of steps is encoded into one command buffer so nothing
    in a step reads back to the browser. Measured on a 1190-atom test system:
    0.8 ms per step, about 215 ns/day in NVT, with an
    energy drift of 0.30 kJ/mol per ns per atom over 20 ps. The viewer
    binds a copy of the same position buffer, so drawing a frame sends nothing to
    the host.

    
Leave Pressure blank for NVT, or give it a value for NPT. The
    barostat is isotropic and synchronises with the browser once per move, which
    costs about 0.3 ms per step.

    
The live analysis follows
    JIGGLE's design, including
    its four-way RMSD split for two-body systems. The one that matters is
    binder fitted on receptor: a whole-complex RMSD averages a
    ligand's drift across every receptor atom that did not move, so a ligand
    coming loose barely registers. Measured here — a binder displaced by
    exactly 0.500 nm shows 0.243 nm in the combined
    trace and 0.500 nm when fitted on the receptor. The
    centroid interface distance misses it too: a 60° rotation of the binder
    about its own centroid moves it by 0.016 nm while the fitted RMSD sees
    0.349 nm.

    
Every metric agrees with MDAnalysis to 2×10−8 nm
    on a trajectory this engine wrote, per-residue RMSF included.

    
What this page does not do: no sticks or cartoons in the
    viewer, no secondary structure, no anisotropic barostat for membranes, and no
    binding free energy. The trajectory it writes is a standard DCD so cpptraj,
    MDAnalysis, VMD and MM/GBSA tooling can do the rest.
